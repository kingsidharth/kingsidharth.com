It's crazy how every start-up wants to be bigger. Usually when I see them presenting their start-ups, there is a lot of talk about 'scaling' and how big the market is. It is one of the biggest turn-offs. Start-ups don't grow up to be bigger ventures but to be better ventures. [Don't go big - specialize, innovate and rule the world.](/notes/small-business-are-better "Don't go big - specialize, innovate and rule the world.") I'd rather have my web-designs start-up to grow into _UI for shopping cart design agency_ than _we-do-it-all design agency_.

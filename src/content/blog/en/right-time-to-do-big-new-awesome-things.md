While expanding my personal notes to these blog posts, I often come across a note which is relatively a new learning. I often catch my self saying, _"I can't write about that. I need to practice it and learn more about it."_ Or _"It's a new idea. I should wait."_ Or even worse, _"It's such a good idea. I will blog about that later."_ Many of us want to put awesome / new things off for later - for the right time. **When are you gonna start-up?** _Later, when the time is right._ **When are you starting to blog?** _Not now, it's going_ _to be_ _good - I am not feeling like it._ … and on it goes. It's fun to challenge that later and do it now! You will be amazed at how good you are. [I believe there is no right or wrong decision; you have the ability to make any decisions the right one.](/notes/make-right-decision-for-start-up "I believe there is no right or wrong decision; you have the ability to make any decisions the right one.") **Extension****:** There is no right or wrong day for anything; you have the ability to make any day the day. If you think it's today - you are right. If you think it is tomorrow - you are right. **Is it today?** Bonus:

*   > "If you think you can do a thing or think you can't do a thing, you're right." -Henry Ford

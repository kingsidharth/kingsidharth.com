Often, entrepreneurs delay a decision for long trying to figure out the 'right' decision. Guess what? There is none! You never get it done (your passion - which can be your start-up) and you never get it wrong. And the reason you never get it wrong is because you never get it done - you can always [improve and be better.](/notes/small-business-are-better) Non-decisive state waters down the passion and you ask _"Dude! I was so passionate about it; but not anymore."_ Just make a decision - making one is important than making a right one or best one. You have the ability to make any decision the right one.

If you are bogged down in doing what you are _supposed_ to do, then…

*   You are not doing enough.
*   You are not being yourself.
*   You are following convection, a rat race.
*   You don't have an opinion.
*   [You don't think you matter](/notes/will-it-matter "You don't think you matter") and you are proving it.
*   You can't [make a decision](/notes/make-right-decision-for-start-up " make a decision").
*   **You are not an entrepreneur.**

Do what you feel for, what you believe in what you were born to do. Be yourself.

Top-p is described as "keep the smallest set of tokens whose probability sums to
p." True, and misleading — because temperature has already rewritten those
probabilities before top-p looks at them.

At `T=0.4` the distribution is spiky, so 90% of the mass lives in about three
tokens. At `T=1.6` the same logits flatten out and you need thirty-odd tokens to
reach the same 90%.

Same `p`. Same model. Ten times the candidate pool.

This is why "we set top-p to 0.9" is not a description of anything. It is half a
setting.

> "...my cat. I Abrahamed (manifested) her." [Abraham Hicks:](http://www.Abraham-Hicks.com) "We've become a verb."

How do you know if your brand has crushed it? How do you know it's about to? Here is a _good_ litmus test: **People love you.** How do you know that people love you? Here is a _great_ litmus test: **You become a verb.** _"I think I will Google it"_ _"I am facebooking / orkuting."_ _"Tweeps / Tweets / Get twitter."_ _"It's an Apple thing."_ _"I Abrahamed her."_ _"You Crushed it!"_ You don't make yourself a verb - that's screwy. You let your customers make you one. And you let them do that by being awesome. It is not "the" ultimate test. but it is a good one. This is another reason to get a brandable name and not keyword thing and another reason to focus on bringing out the product instead of perfecting it.

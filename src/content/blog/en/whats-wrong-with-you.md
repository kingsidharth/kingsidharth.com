So much focus on _"What's wrong with you / this / that / everything."_ Looks like most people are trying to figure or point out what is wrong. Bosses are busy telling that to their employees. Critics are busy telling that about products and mentors to entrepreneurs. **Stop for a moment** and turn the other cheek. Look at what all is right, try to expand it. That is where you will find solutions for what is right.

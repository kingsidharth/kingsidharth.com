It's story of an entrepreneurs from idea to world class execution - and many slaps in between. Guru (antagonist) leaves his job on promotion because he can't work for others, marries to get funded while his father is against it and changes the system to fit him. It is one hell of inspiring movies with nice Bollywood music and dance. Get subtitles if you need to - it's worth it.

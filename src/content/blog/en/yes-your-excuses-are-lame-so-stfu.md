I've a cell phone from a local company which often runs out of battery. It's so amusing to see it say 'Battery Low' and then turn off with loud music! I wonder how long it could have lasted without playing that music. So often people do the same thing, they complain about not having enough time / money / space / courage / ideas and whatever.

Every argument about "which temperature should I use" is really an argument about
a distribution nobody has looked at. This guide puts the distribution on screen
first, then adds one knob at a time.

<SamplingLab />

The bench above is the whole machine, live. Each panel is why the last one
matters: the dial that should matter but doesn't, what was underneath it, the
question that decides whether you are sampling or guessing — then the switches,
run in series the way the real pipeline does. The chapters below take the same
ground in prose, one knob at a time.

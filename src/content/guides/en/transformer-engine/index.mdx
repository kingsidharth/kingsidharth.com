Every large language model — GPT, Claude, Llama, all of them — is the same
machine. Text goes in the bottom as numbers, something comes out the top as a
distribution over what comes next. Everything in between is one block, copied
and stacked.

This is the machine, exploded:

</div>

Drag to orbit it. Scroll to zoom. Tap a part — the first block, the attention
slab, the MLP — and the camera takes you inside, where the block opens up into
its real contents: attention built out of three matrices called Q, K and V,
and an MLP that is just two matrices with a kink between them.

The rest of this guide reads the machine part by part, bottom to top:

1. **Tokenisation** — text becomes integers.
2. **Embeddings** — integers become vectors, and the residual stream begins.
3. **One block** — attention, add & norm, MLP, add & norm. The whole trick.
4. **Attention, opened** — Q, K, V, and what the heads are doing.
5. **The MLP** — the two-thirds of the model nobody diagrams.
6. **The full stack** — unembedding, sampling, and why it loops.

Read it in order. Each part assumes the previous one.
